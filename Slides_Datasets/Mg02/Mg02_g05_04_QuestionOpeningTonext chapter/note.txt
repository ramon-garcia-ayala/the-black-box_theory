The planer doesn't decide what the wood will become. 
It holds open a question. 
And this is exactly where we arrive at one of the most uncomfortable questions in the room right now —
Can a machine hold open a question of its own?
Not just respond to the material. 
Not just transmit signals back to the hand. 
But actually reach into a space that wasn't there before
and pull something out of it that nobody put in.

In other words:

Can AI be creative? Can something truly new emerge from a dataset of existing designs?
