In the case of AI, data centers, and computational infrastructure, are often kept far from view. 
They remain physically and conceptually distant, almost invisible to the people who use them every day.

So back to Simondon, this is how we identify the two opposite yet closely related ways of 
avoiding a genuine understanding of technical objects.

We either ignore them completely, treating them as black boxes whose inner workings do not concern us. like data centers

Or we elevate them to something almost mythical, attributing to them powers they do not actually possess.

In both cases, the result is the same: we fail to understand the machine itself. We either dismiss it or worship it, 
but rarely do we engage with it
